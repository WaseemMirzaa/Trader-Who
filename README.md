# TraderWho

*A modern mobile platform to find service providers, hire professionals, track jobs, and communicate seamlessly.*

---

## Overview

**TraderWho** is a Flutter-based mobile application that connects clients with trusted service providers.
Users can browse services, hire professionals, negotiate custom job prices, track the progress of ongoing tasks, and communicate directly through in-app messaging.

TraderWho simplifies the entire service hiring process—from discovering skilled workers to completing jobs—within one unified platform.

---

## Features

### 🔍 Find Service Providers

* Browse a variety of service categories
* View profiles, ratings, and rates
* Discover professionals based on skills, availability, and location

### 💼 Fixed-Rate Services

* Each service comes with predefined pricing
* Book instantly with transparent cost information
* No hidden fees or indirect negotiations required

### 💬 Custom Jobs

* Create custom job requests with open pricing
* Discuss details and rates directly with service providers
* Agree on terms before the work begins

### 📲 In-App Messaging

* Real-time chat between clients and service providers
* Share job details, images, requirements, or updates
* Maintain secure communication inside the app

### 📦 Order Tracking

* Track job progress from start to completion
* Notifications for each status change
* Review job history and completed orders

### ✔ Job Completion Flow

* Service providers can mark tasks as completed
* Clients confirm and finalize the order
* Transparent transaction history for both parties
